RoboCup 2026 – Middle Size League World Vice-Champions

Competition Overview

I participated in the RoboCup 2026 World Championship in Incheon, South Korea, as a member of Robot Club Toulon.

After a week of competition and several matches against international teams, we finished as RoboCup 2026 Middle Size League World Vice-Champions.

The Middle Size League is a robotic soccer competition in which teams design and develop their own autonomous mobile robots. During each match, the robots must perceive their environment, locate the ball, coordinate with their teammates, make strategic decisions and move autonomously on the field.

Project Development

This result was the outcome of several months of designing, developing, testing and continuously improving the team's robotic systems. Preparing for an international competition required the integration of multiple technical fields into a reliable and fully autonomous system.

The robots combine mechanical design, embedded electronics, real-time software, computer vision, artificial intelligence and multi-robot coordination. Each subsystem must operate reliably while interacting with the rest of the robot during fast and unpredictable matches.

Competition Experience

During the competition, our robots had to operate autonomously in dynamic matches against systems developed by teams from around the world. The event provided a demanding environment for evaluating the robustness, precision and reliability of our developments.

Between matches, the team analyzed robot behavior, identified technical issues and implemented improvements under strict time constraints. This required effective teamwork, rapid problem-solving and strong coordination between the software, electronics and mechanical development activities.
